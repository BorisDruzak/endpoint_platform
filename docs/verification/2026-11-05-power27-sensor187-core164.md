## Rollout controls and future releases

After canaries, at most two assignments reserve installation/observation slots.
An applied target retains its slot until fresh postchecks and at least60 seconds
of observation pass. Failure/rollback blocks new assignments. Canonical service
transactions and audit records are used; target states are never manually
rewritten. Agent recommendation polling can take about five minutes; waiting
for a legitimate poll is part of the test.

This Windows patch is durable for updated agents across their future journal
rollovers. New full Setup media must incorporate the corrected runtime in a
separately versioned and qualified native release; historical installers are not
silently replaced. Existing156/older Foundation sources require their approved
full-Setup path before this minimum158 Core. Historical156 ACL failures are not
requalified by a successful158-to164 Core update. The full-Setup fleet switch
remains disabled/max_parallel1; this explicit Core rollout does not enable it.
Linux is unaffected. Known Yandex telemetry remains user-deferred. READY source
